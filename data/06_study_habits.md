# How I study and work


## Routine
I am a night owl. I prefer studying and coding late at night when the house is quiet and there are no distractions. My routine usually involves attending classes during the day and then taking a break in the late afternoon. I start my actual studying or project work around 8 PM and go until 1 or 2 AM.

## Tools
For writing notes, I mostly use Notion because I can organize my courses into different pages. For coding, I use VS Code, and I rely heavily on GitHub for version control. For team communication, I use WhatsApp and Discord.

## Exam Prep
I start preparing for exams about 10 to 14 days in advance. I go through the lecture slides first and make condensed summaries. Then I solve the past papers, as I find that questions usually repeat in similar formats. 

## Teamwork
When working in a team, I prefer to establish a clear division of work right at the beginning. I usually volunteer to handle the backend or database side. I like to set up the GitHub repository and make sure all team members know how to push and pull code without creating merge conflicts.

## What I Do When Stuck
If I get stuck on a coding problem, I follow a three-step process. First, I read my own code out loud to spot obvious errors. Second, I search on Google or StackOverflow for the exact error message. Third, if I am still stuck, I watch a YouTube tutorial on the specific concept. I try not to ask for the direct solution from friends; I prefer to understand why it's not working.

# My hobbies, tastes and opinions

## Hobbies
When I'm not coding, I spend a lot of time watching tech review videos on YouTube. I also really enjoy playing video games, especially strategy games like Civilization VI, because I love planning and resource management. 

## Favourites
My favorite book is "The Pragmatic Programmer" by Andrew Hunt and David Thomas, which completely changed how I think about writing clean code.
## Values

I value honesty and consistency above everything else. I believe that in software engineering, being reliable and communicating clearly is just as important as writing good code.
## My hot takes

I have a strong opinion that learning the fundamentals of computer science (like data structures and algorithms) is way more important than just memorizing the syntax of the newest trending framework. Frameworks change, but the core logic stays the same.
## Advice I would give a junior student
